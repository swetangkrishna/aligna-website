01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

Fitness apps track activity.
Aligna builds context.

Workout history, food, recovery, progress and wearable data usually live in separate silos. The result is tracking without a coherent model of the person.

Aligna is being built as a continuously adapting fitness intelligence layer that connects those signals and turns them into useful next actions.

01

Generic plans

Static programs ask the user to adapt to the plan.

→Aligna adapts the plan.
02

Fragmented data

Training, food, body and wearables sit in different places.

→Aligna connects the signals.
03

Metrics without action

Dashboards explain what happened, not what to do next.

→Aligna turns context into decisions.
