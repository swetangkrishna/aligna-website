01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

Fitness apps track activity.
Aligna understands context.

People do not train, recover, eat or progress in the same way. Yet most digital fitness products still rely on static plans, fragmented metrics and generic recommendations.

Aligna is being built as a unified personal fitness intelligence layer that continuously learns from the user's body, behaviour and progress — then adapts the experience around them.

01

Generic planning

Static programs make the user adapt to the plan.

→Aligna adapts the plan to the user.
02

Disconnected tracking

Workout, nutrition and progress data live in separate silos.

→Aligna connects the signals.
03

Numbers without meaning

Dashboards show metrics but rarely explain what to do next.

→Aligna turns progress into action.

04 / WHY NOW

The next generation of fitness software will be personal, multimodal and adaptive.

01

AI is moving from features to agents. Users increasingly expect software to interpret context and help them decide what to do next.

02

Phones are becoming capable AI devices. On-device intelligence creates new possibilities for lower-latency, privacy-conscious personalisation.

03

Fitness data is abundant but fragmented. The opportunity is not simply more tracking — it is better interpretation across signals.
